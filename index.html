04 / A little more Gab

Curiosity is
my operating system.

I’m drawn to systems with depth—whether that’s a game build, a PC setup, a racing model, or a product idea. I like knowing what moves the needle, what could break, and how the pieces affect one another.

I don’t enjoy surface-level answers. I like the research, trade-offs, and iterative tuning that turn a good idea into a reliable one. That instinct follows me from hardware and gaming into every product I build.
